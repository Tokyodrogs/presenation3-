SECTION I

Executive Summary

Revolutionizing Philippine MSME inventory with native voice AI.

The Problem

Over 68% of Filipino SMEs still rely on manual pen-and-paper or basic spreadsheets for daily stock counting.

₱2.3B Annual lost revenue across PH due to stockouts & spoilage
  • Severe stockouts & dead capital overstocking
  • Manual entry fatigue & human logging errors

The Solution

VOXSTOCK is a voice-first, AI-driven inventory platform that lets store owners and warehouse staff manage stock hands-free.

Tagalog • Cebuano • English Real-time Cloud + Offline Sync

Simply talk to your phone or tablet while unpacking or stocking shelves.

Key Value Proposition

Zero learning curve. No typing required. No expensive barcode scanners needed.

97.4% Voice recognition accuracy for local Filipino accents
"Just speak — and your inventory updates instantly in real time."
